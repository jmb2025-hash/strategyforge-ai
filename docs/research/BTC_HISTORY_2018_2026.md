## The cycles in plain terms

- **2018 bear market.** From the December 2017 peak, BTC fell 81% within 2018 ($17,235 high to $3,122 low). It was above its 50-day average on only 20% of days and never above its 200-day average once that average existed. Sharp counter-rallies of +44% to +67% kept failing.
- **2019 recovery.** BTC rose +306% from the December 2018 low to $12,921 in June 2019, then gave back about half by December.
- **2020 crash and bull run.** It fell 53% in four weeks into the March 12 2020 COVID crash, then rose +740% in 302 days to $40,667 in January 2021. This was the strongest trend in the data.
- **2021 double top.** It went to $63,564 in April, then fell 53%. It made a marginal new high of $67,559 in November, which began the next bear market.
- **2022 bear market.** It fell 67% within the year: the 60% drop of May–June (the Luna and 3AC collapse), then the FTX low of $15,766 in November. It was above its 200-day average on 0% of days.
- **2023–2024 bull market.** It rose +364% over 478 days to $73,121 in March 2024 and corrected 26% over the summer. After the election it rose +97% to $106,187 in December 2024. This period was calm: about 21–26 days a year moved 5% or more, against 75 in 2018.
- **2025 top.** After a 28% correction to April, it rose +64% to the all-time high of $124,728 (close; $126,272 intraday) on Oct 6 2025.
- **2025–2026 bear market.** It fell 50% over 122 days to $62,795 (Feb 2026), bounced +31%, then fell to $58,526 (Jun 30 2026, intraday low $57,735).
- **Now.** It has rallied +48% from the June low to $86,587 (Sep 21), and is at $84,667 on Oct 3 2026. It has been above its 50-day average since Aug 16 2026 but is still below its 200-day average (above it on only 17% of 2026 days).

## What this means for strategies

- **The market switches regimes several times a year.** Rules fitted to one regime fail in the next. The 4-hour pullback plan looked excellent in 2025–2026 (83% wins) but lost money in 5 of the 7 earlier years. Chart Champions v3 was profitable in only 4 of 9 years.
- **Trend following captures the big moves but rarely wins.** Daily EMA 9/21, long and short, made money in 6 of 9 years and multiplied capital about 10 times from 2018 (a full position on every trade, costs included), but only about 32% of its trades won. Its losing years were 2018, 2022 and 2025.
- **Trading pullbacks in the trend's direction gives a high win rate across all regimes.** Buy sharp dips above the 40–60-day average; sell sharp rallies below it. About 70–75% of trades won, and 7 of 9 years were profitable. The losing years were 2018 and 2024.
